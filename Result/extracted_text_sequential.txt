Abstract

USMSecure is a mobile application developed to enhance campus safety for students of
Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) in the main campus. The application is designed to address

common safety challenges faced by students, especially during nighttime travel and in

isolated or low-visibility areas. By leveraging mobile technology and real-time data,
USMSecure provides a centralized platform that supports proactive and responsive campus
safety management, aligning with Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 11: Sustainable

5 Proposed Solution
1.5.1 Enhancing Emergency Preparedness and Response

‘To overcome the lack of knowledge and preparedness among students during
emergencies, USMSecure introduces an SOS Button that allows users to seek immediate

help. When activated, the button instantly shares the user's real-time location with campus

security or emergency contacts,

suring rapid assistance even when the user is panicked or
unable to make a call. This system not only improves emergency response time but also

builds a sense of confidence and security among students

1.5.2 Preventing Accidents and Ensuring Safer Campus Navigation

‘To prevent unwanted incidents and enhance preventive safety awareness, USMSecure
features a Best Route Calculation system. By analysing real-time data, user feedback, and
historical safety reports, the system identifies and recommends safer walking routes within

and around campus. Paths that are poorly lit, isolated, or marked as unsafe are avoided,

reducing the likelihood of students encountering dangerous situatio

hours of low-visibilty conditions.

1.5.3 Strengthening Community Engagement and Feedback Systems

‘To improve communication and collective participation in campus safety, USMSecure
includes a Community Hub feature. This interactive platiorm allows users to report unsafe

areas, lostand-found cases, or minor inciden

as well as share and receive real-time updates
